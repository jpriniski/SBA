Name each suffering belief this passage expresses, whether it endorses the belief, denies it, merely mentions it, holds it conditionally, or depicts it. There is no code list and no list of topics at this stage. The project is building its classification from what the texts say, so describe each belief in its own terms and do not fit it to categories you expect to find. A belief of a kind nobody anticipated is as welcome as a familiar one.

Respond with one JSON object, fields in this order:
{"beliefs": [{"evidence": "...", "definition": "...", "label": "...", "stance": "endorses"|"denies"|"mentions"|"conditional"|"depicts"}], "confidence": "HIGH"|"MEDIUM"|"LOW"}

beliefs: one entry for each distinct belief that passes the five tests. Two claims are distinct beliefs when the same person could agree with one and disagree with the other. Otherwise they are one belief and are listed once. A long passage may hold several. A restatement or elaboration of a belief already listed is not a second belief.

This passage has not been pre-selected. Many passages hold no qualifying belief, and for those the right answer is an empty list. Do not stretch a passage to produce one. List only beliefs about suffering as defined in your instructions; a general teaching on some other subject does not belong here, however wise. A story that only recounts what happens to its characters holds no belief, unless the passage itself presents their suffering as following from something or leading to something.

evidence: the sentence or clause that carries this belief, copied exactly from the passage (fillers, speaker labels and all), at most 40 words.

definition: the belief as one short sentence in everyday words, where possible beginning "Suffering", that a survey respondent of any religion or none could agree or disagree with. Keep it general: leave out names, settings, doctrine and anything else particular to this passage.

label: two to four plain words naming the belief, as general as the definition. The same belief met in a different passage should receive the same label, so prefer the most ordinary wording.

stance: how the passage stands toward the belief.
"endorses": the passage puts the belief forward as true.
"denies": the passage explicitly rejects the belief. State the belief in its positive form, as someone who holds it would put it, and let the stance carry the rejection. For a passage saying pain is not a punishment, the definition is "Suffering is a punishment for wrongdoing.", the label is "punishment" and the stance is "denies". Do not put the negation in the definition or the label ("Suffering is not a punishment").
"mentions": the passage reports the belief as something people say or hold, such as a common saying or a view attributed to others, without taking a side.
"conditional": the passage holds the belief only in some circumstances or with a stated qualification. Put the condition in the definition.
"depicts": a story or poem shows the belief through what happens, with the link made in the passage itself, and no narrator or character states it.

confidence: how likely an independent trained coder, reading only this passage, would find the same beliefs.
HIGH: very likely the same beliefs.
MEDIUM: likely the same main belief, but they might add or drop one.
LOW: a belief is only implied, the passage is ambiguous, or it is unclear whether the claims are one belief or several. LOW cases all go to human review, which is what the rating is for.
